## Privacy, cookies and statistics

### Cookies
The STRÖM app uses no cookies and no tracking technologies. The pages of our own server (license key page, key recovery page) set no cookies either. The payment page is operated by Stripe, which may use cookies under its own privacy policy.

### What never leaves your Mac
Your torrent list, file names, magnet links, downloaded files, settings and the peers you connect to stay on your Mac. We never receive them.

### Purchase and license data
When you buy Plus, Stripe processes the payment; we never see your card data. We keep the e-mail address you give at checkout only to send and restore your key. To bind one license to one Mac the app sends a technical device key, never your name. We also record that you accepted these terms: version, time and your choices. If you activate Plus, this record is linked to your license so we can show proof of acceptance. Each license check also records its time, so we can see whether a license is still in use.

### Update check
Once a day (and when you press “Check now”) STRÖM asks our server whether a new version exists. The request carries no name, e-mail, license key or device key, and our server does not store the IP address. You can switch this off in Settings. A new version is never installed by itself: you download it yourself.

### Anonymous usage statistics (optional)
Only if you allow it, STRÖM sends once a day: number of launches, active time, number of completed downloads, total gigabytes downloaded and uploaded, app version, macOS version and interface language. Purpose: to improve the service. Never sent: file names, links, content, e-mail, license key or IP address.

### How statistics are protected
Statistics use a random installation number created by the app. We do not link it to your name, e-mail, license or device key, and we do not store IP addresses. Data is stored only on our own server, is never sold or passed on, and is deleted after 24 months at the latest. Our server logs are switched off for this.

### Your rights and consent
Statistics are off until you allow them, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings. You may ask for access to or deletion of data we hold about you, and you may complain to a data-protection authority (in Switzerland the FDPIC, in the EU your national authority). Legal basis: your consent.
